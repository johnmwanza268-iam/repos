Food in Malawi - chicken - green plate

Description

To prepare the meal, you need the following ingredients: maize flour and water

ingredients

  • 1kg of maize flour
  • 1 cup of water
  • a Pot
  • cooking stick for stiring
  • cooking spoon for serving

Steps

  1. Prepare fire
  2. Pour 1 cup of water into a pot.
  3. Place the pot on fire and wait for the water to get warm.
  4. Once warm, take handful of flour and add to the water.
  5. Stir the water mixer until the flour mixes properly with the water.
  6. Wait for the porridge to boil for 5 minutes.
  7. add more flour while stiring until it hardens to your liking.
  8. Then serve the dish and enjoy.
